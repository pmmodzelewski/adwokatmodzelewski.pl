## Ignorance of the Law Is No Excuse

<i>Ignorantia iuris nocet</i> – this maxim, rooted in ancient Roman law, is commonly translated as "ignorance of the law is no excuse" (or, literally, "ignorance of the law harms"). The principle it enshrines holds that being unaware of one's obligations or rights does not relieve one of the duty to comply with the former, nor does it place upon anyone else the responsibility of ensuring that one is able to exercise the latter.

## Soldier, Police Officer, Uniformed Officer – Know Your Rights!

Service in the uniformed forces is one of the most demanding career paths one can undertake – the stakes are exceptionally high, the stress considerable, the risks serious, and the constant threat to health and life is an inherent part of daily duties. A sound knowledge of the law, an understanding of one's entitlements and obligations, and a clear grasp of one's legal position are essential to safeguarding one's interests – both in avoiding liability and in fully availing oneself of the legitimate privileges with which the State recognises the daily sacrifices made by those in service.
