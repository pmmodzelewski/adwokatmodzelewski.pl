**Succession — that is, the situation in which, following the death of a testator, an heir steps into all or part of his rights and obligations — comprises two essential elements. The first is determining who is entitled to inherit (whether under statute or by will); the second is the acceptance or renunciation of the inheritance by that person.**

## What options does an heir have?

A person who stands to inherit an estate has one of three options: they may accept it outright (unconditionally), accept it with the benefit of inventory (z dobrodziejstwem inwentarza), or renounce it.

The first option is the most straightforward. It results in both the rights (primarily, though not exclusively, rights of ownership in respect of, for example, real property, movable assets, and money) and the obligations (crucially, debts and liabilities are also inherited) that the deceased held at the date of death passing to the heir in the relevant proportion.

Renunciation of the inheritance means, as provided by the Civil Code (Kodeks cywilny), that the heir is treated as though they did not survive the testator. They are thereby excluded from the circle of those entitled to inherit, and their place is taken by other persons in accordance with the provisions of the Code — or of the will, if it contained substitution provisions in the event of the death of the person named therein as heir.

The most complex option is acceptance with the benefit of inventory. This limits the heir's liability for the debts of the estate to the value of the inherited assets. By way of example: if a person is to inherit a flat worth 600,000 PLN together with debts totalling 1,000,000 PLN, their liability would be restricted solely to the sum of 600,000 PLN.

## Time Limits

An heir has **6 months** from the date on which they became aware of their call to succession in which to submit a declaration of acceptance (in any form) or renunciation of the inheritance. What is relevant, therefore, is the heir's subjective awareness that they stand to inherit, rather than any objective event (such as the date of the testator's death) — although, for a child of the deceased who learns of the death on the very day it occurs, the time limit will naturally begin to run immediately.

Should no declaration be made within that period, the heir is deemed to have accepted the inheritance with the benefit of inventory.

## The Last Heir

Every estate must be inherited by someone. Should all heirs — whether testamentary or statutory — renounce the inheritance, the last heir of resort (who may not renounce it) is the State Treasury or the municipality (gmina) in whose territory the deceased was resident at the time of death.
