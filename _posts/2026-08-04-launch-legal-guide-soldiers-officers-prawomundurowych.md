## Ignorance of the Law Is No Defence

<i>Ignorantia iuris nocet</i> – this maxim, rooted in ancient Roman law, is typically translated as "ignorance of the law is no defence" (or "ignorance of the law harms"). The principle underlying it holds that our unawareness of our obligations or rights does not mean we are excused from complying with the former, nor that it is someone else's responsibility to ensure we are able to exercise the latter.

## Soldier, Police Officer, Uniformed Officer – Know Your Rights!

Service in the uniformed forces is one of the most demanding career paths one can choose – the stakes are exceptionally high, the stress considerable, the risks serious, and the threat to health and life a constant feature of daily duties. A sound knowledge of the law, an understanding of one's entitlements and obligations, and clarity about one's legal position are essential for protecting one's interests – avoiding liability, whilst at the same time fully exercising the legitimate privileges with which the State reciprocates the daily sacrifices made by those who serve.
