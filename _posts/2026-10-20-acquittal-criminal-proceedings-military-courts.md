**After proceedings lasting nearly four years, the firm's client was acquitted by a final and binding judgment of**
**charges relating to a criminal offence he was alleged to have committed in the course of, and in connection with,**
**his service as a professional soldier.**

## Background to the Case

The firm's client had served as a professional soldier for over a decade. During the final stage of his service, he was — as it transpired, wrongly — suspected of misappropriating property belonging to the Armed Forces. It was alleged that over a period of several months he had misappropriated items with a total value in excess of 100,000 PLN and had subsequently sold them in order to generate a regular income.

The charges brought against the client were serious. In addition to the risk of an unconditional custodial sentence and an obligation to make good the alleged loss, he also faced demotion in rank. In the course of the proceedings, the client decided to leave the service.

## Details of the Proceedings

The first-instance proceedings were conducted before the Military Garrison Court (Wojskowy Sąd Garnizonowy) in Warsaw. The course of the proceedings was at times turbulent and the case was extensive in scope: several dozen witnesses were examined, expert opinion evidence was obtained from a range of specialists, and specialist information was sought from a number of professional institutions. Approximately thirty hearing dates were required before the first-instance court delivered its judgment.

The Military Garrison Court itself found that the body of evidence gathered did not provide grounds for finding the client guilty of the offence with which he had been charged. The detailed written grounds for the judgment noted, amongst other matters, the complete absence of any concerns on the part of the client's superiors, colleagues and other persons regarding the manner in which he had discharged his service duties — a state of affairs that would not have been possible had he in fact been committing the alleged offence. The legal nuances raised in the course of the defence also carried significant weight.

## The Judgment and Its Consequences

The Prosecution lodged an appeal against the first-instance judgment. The Military Regional Court (Wojskowy Sąd Okręgowy) in Warsaw, however, dismissed the appeal as unfounded and upheld the judgment under challenge. This means that the client will not only avoid any criminal liability for the alleged offence — which entails, amongst other consequences, the possibility of returning to service, the absence of any penalty, and no obligation to repay substantial sums of money — but he is also entitled to seek reimbursement of the costs of his defence. This is unquestionably the best possible outcome and the most favourable conclusion to the proceedings that one could have hoped for.

## Legal Representation for Professional Soldiers

Criminal defence work forms one of the cornerstones of my practice as a lawyer specialising in matters concerning professional soldiers and officers of other uniformed services. Given the particular nature of military service, the consequences of even wholly unsubstantiated charges can be extremely serious, including for a client's career. For this reason, seeking professional legal assistance at the earliest stages of any proceedings is strongly advisable.
